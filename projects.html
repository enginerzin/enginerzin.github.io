Research funding

Funded Projects

My research has been supported by national and international funding agencies, as well as industry collaborations. These projects span speech and audio signal processing, multimedia analysis, machine learning, affective computing, human–robot interaction, and AI-driven applications. They reflect a sustained effort to advance fundamental research while developing practical solutions through interdisciplinary and industry partnerships.
