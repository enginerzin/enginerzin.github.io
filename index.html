About

Human-centered signal processing and machine intelligence.

I received my B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees in Electrical Engineering from Bilkent University in 1990, 1992, and 1995. After a postdoctoral fellowship at the Signal Compression Laboratory, University of California, Santa Barbara, I joined Lucent Technologies, where I worked in speech and audio technologies. I have been with Koç University since 2001.

My current research spans speech and audio processing, continuous emotion recognition, multimodal learning, social human–robot interaction, video understanding, and language models for specialized domains.

Research

Current directions

Selected themes connecting signal processing, machine learning, and human-centered AI.

01

Speech & Affective Computing

Speech emotion recognition, continuous affect prediction, self-supervised speech representations, and robust acoustic modeling.

02

Multimodal Human Behavior

Audio, speech, face, body movement, and temporal cross-modal alignment for understanding human affect and engagement.

03

Social Human–Robot Interaction

Learning expressive robot behavior, backchannels, engagement models, and human-aligned facial expressions.

04

Video Understanding

Video summarization, annotation consensus, representation learning, and evaluation under subjective human annotations.

05

Language Models

Domain-adapted language models and evidence-grounded AI for Turkish legal text, contract analysis, and specialized NLP.

06

EEG & Immersive Systems

EEG-based modeling of human state, including early prediction of cybersickness in immersive virtual environments.
